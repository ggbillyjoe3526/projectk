# Concept shots, round 2: Lamplight (B), gritty and realistic

William picked direction B, with a monochrome look for low health or Resolve, and asked for something grittier, more realistic and less blocky. Round 2 rebuilt the six moments from round 1 at full resolution, each in a normal and a low-Resolve version.

**William's verdict (2026-10-10):** "Better but not quite there." It lost the PS1/PS2 look he wants, low resolution and dithering.

**Removed (2026-10-10):** the frames and the tool for this round are no longer in the repository. Every character's face was a third-party photo-scanned head (CC BY 3.0), and the image lighting used photographed HDR skies, so William asked for them to come out. Round 3 keeps this round's sculpted bodies, textures and scenes, replaces the face and skies with self-made ones, and puts them through direction B's lo-fi pipeline: see [concept-shots-v3](../concept-shots-v3/README.md).

## What this round introduced (kept in round 3)

- **Bodies and clothes** sculpted as smooth shapes (signed distance fields) and meshed at 5 to 8 mm detail: coats with folds, slits and collars, knitwear, scarves, caps, oilskins and boots.
- **Procedural PBR textures** (colour, normal, roughness, metalness) at 512 to 1024 px: damp plaster with tide lines and flaking, harled render, worn floorboards, rusted ship paint, wet flagstones and tarmac, slate, tweed, knitwear, oilskin and coffin wood.
- **Lighting**: soft shadowed lamps, candles and the phone torch, with ambient occlusion (GTAO), bloom, ACES tone mapping, vignette and film grain.
- **The low-Resolve grade**: the world drains to a cold haar grey and only strong reds keep their colour (blood, rowan berries, warning lights). Skin is excluded so faces don't flush red.
