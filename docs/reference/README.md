# Inspiration images

At the start of the project (2026-10-09) William shared seven screenshots as visual inspiration. He was clear they are
inspiration, not a spec, and that the game must not copy any of them.

The images themselves are not in this repository. They are screenshots of other people's games, and this repository is
public. They are kept in the project's shared files.

| Game | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Kuon (PS2) | A high-angle fixed camera, a wooden temple veranda, a warm key light, melee with a pale creature |
| Dead Cells | 2D pixel-art side-scroller: a shrine and statue with candles, full HUD |
| Resident Evil (remaster) | A fixed high camera, a mansion interior lit by a fireplace |
| Bloodborne | A gothic city by moonlight, fog and lamp posts |
| Signalis | Top-down, pixel-look 3D: a hospital ward, gunfire and blood |
| A lo-fi PS1-style 3D game | A tiled washroom corridor under fluorescent lights, a figure in a black suit, a heavy dark vignette |
| A lo-fi PS1-style 3D game (monochrome) | A concrete alley with arches, a third-person chase camera, visible texture pixels |

What they share: a low internal resolution, one strong warm key light against deep shadow, high-angle authored cameras
(four of the seven), and tight interiors.
